Vee Research Assistant

A private research connection between the account owner's Codex assistant and Pinterest's official developer tools.

Setup in progress. This integration is not connected or approved. Available features will depend on Pinterest's approval and the permissions it provides.

Purpose

The intended use is to understand search interest, keyword growth and seasonality for internal product research. Research signals do not establish sales, profitability or future demand.

Access

The integration is intended only for Vee, the owner of @anvikv24. It is not a service that other people can join or connect their accounts to.

Requested use is read-only. Publishing content, changing campaigns, spending on ads and accessing other users' private information are outside its scope.

How it would work

Following approval, the owner would authorize access through Pinterest. Codex would submit permitted research requests and explain the returned results. The exact data access must be verified before the connection is enabled.

This project is independently operated by Vee. It is not endorsed by Pinterest or OpenAI.
